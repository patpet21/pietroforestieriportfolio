## Case studies
All seven remaining projects now use the compact split-screen treatment pioneered on PrivacyGate, while PrivacyGate itself is preserved.
- PrivateStone — verified request/proposal workflow (concept, not a public operating marketplace)
- NYC Retail IT Coordination — operational/vendor coordination, without fabricating sensitive internal client screenshots
- Business Websites & Booking — visitor, service and booking journey concepts
- Lead Rescue — early prototype; concept visuals only
- E-Learning & AI PM Lab — original brand assets and learning prototype, no invented completion data
- PropertyDEX — real-estate tokenization research; not a live investment product
- PropertyDEX Framework — structured evaluation and decision-support exploration

Each layout has a smaller hero, a dedicated logo, a factual challenge statement, role, optional external site, tabs for Visual / Approach / Current stage, and an interactive gallery where multiple original images exist. Each page also shows three process steps and tighter later sections.
